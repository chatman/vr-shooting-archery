The playing hall is a room with:
* 6 meters of floor to ceiling height.
* 30 meters width
* 42 meters in length
* The back wall has wood colour, and 40 targets of 10 meters air pistol (exact specs added at the end), spaced 1 meter apart, at a height of 1.5m from the floor.
* Every target is enclosed within a green box with SIUS written on it. See sius.jpg file for reference.
* There's a very long table along the entire length of the room, at a distance of 10 meters from the back wall. The height of the table is 1m from the floor, is wooden in colour/texture, and is a boxy shaped one (legs are not visible). The table top has length 40m and depth of 1m.
* The floor colour is dark green between the table and the back wall, and black matte in front of the row of tables.
* In front of the table, there are 40 chairs kept, 1 meter from each other and about 2 meters in front of the table.
* The ceiling has rectangular lights all over the place so that the whole playing hall is well lit.
* Let the front of the room have 4 doors from where players and audience members can enter. The wall be toughened glass.
* Every target should have a lane number written on top of it (black on yellow, about 1 meter above the target) starting from 1 to 40. It should be large enough so that it is clearly legible from the back of the room.
* On the front side of the table, corresponding lane/firing point numbers should be written (1 to 40).

Overall Card & Layout
----------------------
Card Size: Minimum 170 mm × 170 mm square.
Color Scheme: Rings 1 through 6 are white; rings 7 through 10 form the black center aiming mark.

Ring Diameters
---------------
10-Ring: 11.5 mm
Inner-Ten Circle: 5.0 mm (used as a tie-breaker)
9-Ring: 27.5 mm
8-Ring: 43.5 mm
7-Ring (Start of black): 59.5 mm
6-Ring: 75.5 mm
Rings 5 down to 1: Scale outward incrementally (each ring increases radius by 8 mm per side, meaning diameter increases by 16 mm per step).
Ring Line Thickness: 0.1 mm to 0.2 mm
